📈 Project: Ghana Adventures Business Dashboard

The Problem: Developed an interactive Business Intelligence dashboard to analyze booking trends, customer behaviour, revenue performance, and tour package performance, enabling management to make data-driven decisions and improve business operations.

Tools Used: Microsoft Excel (Power Query), Power BI

Key Insight: Peak seasons generated the highest bookings and revenue, while repeat customers demonstrated higher average spending, highlighting the importance of customer retention strategies and targeted off-season marketing campaigns.
